## Scope

Whole prism-api admin console: shell (layout/nav), sign-in and setup, dashboard, clients list, client detail, groups, settings and DNS profiles, and the public home page. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and job

A technical parent, possibly with a second adult admin, on desktop or phone. Quick check-ins (which PCs are home, who is on them, what is on screen, send a note) and occasional setup work (groups, DNS profiles, versions). It must work equally well on a phone.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Every family PC is a tagged key on the hall rack: at a glance you see which are home, whose they are, and who had them last. It refuses the neutral-sidebar admin with stat tiles and a nine-column table carrying a form in every row.

OWN-WORLD: Painted-hall slate rail (#1E2B2F) with a brass-yellow active key; cool plaster ground; iron ink; 1px hairlines carry all structure (no shadows, no nested cards). Each client owns a steel-rimmed colour tag (red, yellow, blue, green, orange, violet, picked deterministically from its id) with its name on black embossed label-maker tape. Offline tags drop off the hook and desaturate. Stale is a yellow tape strip.

STORY: The admin sees which PCs are home and what needs attention, acts in one tap (message, screenshot, DNS), and leaves trusting the state.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A slate rail with nav and counts (bottom tab bar on phones). The header sentence reads "N of M PCs are home". The full-width rack shows a hook bar with tags hanging; offline tags sit lower. Below that, a needs-attention list sits beside a quick message composer. The primary action is Send.

FORM: Key Rack, candidate 5 of 7; seed key 4b2420e5. Raises: 1px hairline-only structure (annual), fixed personal furniture per client (character), a fixed type and status ramp (atlas), and a written state vocabulary for every control (centre rail).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
